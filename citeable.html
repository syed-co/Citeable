Common questions

Everything you need
to know about AEO

Straight answers — written the way AI engines prefer to extract them.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization — is the practice of structuring your brand so AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews cite you when someone asks a question related to your business. It's also called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) or LLM Optimization. The discipline is different from traditional SEO: instead of chasing rankings on a results page, you're building the signals AI engines use to decide whose name gets said out loud when a buyer asks for a recommendation. Those signals are mostly off-site — web mentions, brand anchors, YouTube presence — not your Google rank.

Research on 75,000 brands by Ahrefs found the top three predictors of AI citation are all off-site: third-party web mentions (correlation 0.664), brand anchor links from external sites (0.527), and brand search volume — how many people are already searching your name (0.392 for AI Overviews, and the #1 predictor for ChatGPT specifically). YouTube mentions showed the single strongest correlation of all at 0.737 across ChatGPT, AI Mode, and AI Overviews — stronger than backlinks, domain authority, or any on-site factor. On-site, FAQ schema, Organization schema, answer-first content, and a clean llms.txt are the highest-impact technical fixes. Both layers matter — but most agencies only touch the on-site work.

Related but different. Gemini is Google's underlying AI model — their standalone AI assistant, comparable to ChatGPT. Google AI Overviews are the AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of Google Search results, powered by Gemini. Google AI Mode is a newer, more conversational search experience that also runs on Gemini. Think of Gemini as the engine and AI Overviews / AI Mode as two of the surfaces it powers. Perplexity and ChatGPT operate their own separate models entirely. We audit and optimize for all of them.

Not automatically. Google itself has confirmed that indexing no longer guarantees AI search visibility — they're separate systems with separate signals. That said, there's overlap: 76% of AI Overview citations do pull from top-10 ranking pages, so strong SEO helps. But ChatGPT and Perplexity operate independently of Google rankings entirely and draw heavily from training data, third-party mentions, and real-time web search. A business can rank #1 on Google and be completely absent from ChatGPT — and vice versa. AEO addresses the signals that traditional SEO misses.

On-site changes — FAQ schema, Organization markup, llms.txt — are often picked up within days by AI crawlers once live. Measurable improvement in citation frequency typically appears within 4–8 weeks of completing The Setup. Off-site authority compounds more slowly: PR mentions, directory presence, and YouTube citations build over months, which is why the retainer exists. AI engines also prefer fresher content — research shows they consistently favour more recently updated pages over older ones, so staying current is a compounding advantage.

The Retainer builds off-site authority and monitors your citation share over time — but that only compounds if the on-site foundation is correct first. If your schema is wrong, your robots.txt is blocking AI crawlers, or your core pages don't have the content structure AI engines need, off-site work won't land. The Setup fixes all of that.
